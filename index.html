05Next

Where this is heading

Data-parallel training is signal averaging. Every GPU computes a noisy estimate of the true gradient; all-reduce averages them, and the noise falls by the square root of the worker count. The hard part is doing that across thousands of accelerators without the network becoming the bottleneck, which is exactly where my networking work and my ML work meet.
